Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Who we are

This policy explains how Solid Tracking (“we”, “us”) handles the details people share with us when they enquire about our vehicle tracking services.

What we collect

When you make an enquiry we collect:

  • Company name
  • Contact number
  • City
  • Number of vehicles
  • Service needed

How we receive it

You may give us these details through:

  • Our website
  • WhatsApp
  • Phone calls
  • Google Ads lead forms

If you submit a Google Ads lead form, Google shares the details you entered in that form with us.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry and to send you a quote.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 12 months after our last contact with you, then delete them.

Asking us to delete your data

To ask us to delete your details, send us a message on WhatsApp at +966 54 204 3869. Tell us the company name and contact number you used so we can find your record.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above.
